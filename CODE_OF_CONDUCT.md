# Code of Conduct

jpm welcomes contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, and ways of working. This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces, and to people representing the project.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Discuss ideas and code without attacking people. Ask for context before assuming bad intent. Give newcomers useful explanations. Accept corrections, respect boundaries, and keep feedback focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory or sexualized language, personal attacks, unwanted contact after a request to stop, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable. Spam and repeated disruption of project discussions are also not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **carbon1024@foxmail.com**. Include relevant links and a description of what happened; share only the personal information necessary to assess the report. Do not publish sensitive details in an issue.

Reports will be handled with discretion. Information may be shared with the people needed to investigate or respond; absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Do not retaliate against someone for making a good-faith report.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may ask for a correction, remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban participation, depending on the conduct and its impact. Serious threats or repeated violations may result in an immediate restriction.

You may request reconsideration by replying to the reporting address with relevant new context. This is a small, maintainer-led project, and it does not currently have an independent appeals panel.
